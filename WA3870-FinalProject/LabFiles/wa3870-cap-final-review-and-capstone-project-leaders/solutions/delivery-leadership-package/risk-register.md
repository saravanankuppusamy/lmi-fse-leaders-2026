## How I'll use this register

I re-read it at the start of each daily check-in and again after each inject; a risk that fires moves to the top of that day's status update with its trigger named. R3 fired sideways on Wednesday: the environment that broke was the shared demo cluster, which this register had already walked the launch away from. The register lives in the repo so Friday's managers can see what I was watching, not just what went wrong.
